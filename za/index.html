Follow the stops in order. Each Pokémon is listed at the earliest point you can get it, based on when its Wild Zone unlocks in the main missions. Tap a row to check it off. Evolves into shows later stages and how they evolve.

Shiny Charm: in Legends: Z-A it isn't a reward for finishing the Pokédex. You get it at Research Level 50 in Mable's Pokédex research, which counts catching, battling and other research tasks. Filling the Pokédex earns a big share of those points.

Mega Stones

Each Mega Stone lets one Pokémon Mega Evolve. You can only use them once you have the Mega Ring from the main story (Main Mission 10). Every stone is a one-time pickup, so check each one off as you get it.

  • Most stones are sold in shops for Mega Shards or found around the city's districts. Hyperspace stones need the Mega Dimension DLC.
  • A few come from online ranked battles or Mystery Gift events, which may be time-limited.
  • Catch one first means you haven't checked off that Pokémon in the Pokédex tab yet.
