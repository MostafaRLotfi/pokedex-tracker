Follow the stops in order. They follow the usual recommended order of Gyms, Titans and Team Star bases by level. Each Pokémon is listed at the earliest point it shows up, based on its lowest wild level. Tap a row to check it off. Evolves into shows later stages and how they evolve.

Shiny Charm: catch all 400 Pokémon in the Paldea Pokédex, then see Jacq in the Academy's Biology Lab. You'll need trades for the other version's exclusives.

Ominous Stakes

Each of the four Treasures of Ruin is sealed in a shrine. Pull all eight stakes of a color to open its shrine, then battle and catch the legendary inside (level 60). Every stake is a one-time pickup.

  • Stakes glow, so they're easiest to spot at night. They're all in the open world, close to their shrine's region.
  • Purple opens the Grasswither Shrine (Wo-Chien), yellow the Icerend Shrine (Chien-Pao), blue the Firescourge Shrine (Chi-Yu) and green the Groundblight Shrine (Ting-Lu).
